Entering Location Fields

Type the exact or standard names of the State, District, and Court Complex. Leading and trailing spaces are automatically trimmed.

Example:
State: Delhi
District: New Delhi
Court Complex: Patiala House Courts
Order Date Criteria

Enter the date range in DD-MM-YYYY format. You can click the calendar icon to use the system date picker.

  • Interim Orders: Orders issued during proceedings.
  • Final Orders: Concluding judgments and decrees.
  • Both: Retrieves all available orders.
CAPTCHA Verification

When you click Go, the automated browser connects to the portal. If the official portal presents a CAPTCHA challenge, the challenge image will be displayed on screen. Type the characters into the *Enter Captcha box and submit to continue.

Official Document Links

When results are found, click Open Official Order. It opens the authentic eCourts PDF link in a new secure browser tab. No copies are saved locally.
